Proof of Life

A .pol file is a lived-experience record you keep beside a song, essay, photograph, or other work: who stayed with it, how long, where if you choose, what was hard, and — only if you add them — a few body signals from a watch or a CSV.

Create · View · No server · No covert capture
The open format and verifier now live at OpenPOL. This archive keeps a working copy of the minting tool. The product home of the .pol format is https://laudsama.github.io/openpol/ (intended public URL; use the sibling folder ../openpol until that site is published).

01What this will not prove

A new file extension is easy. Cryptographic proof that a human sweated over a work is not — not from a static webpage, and not from numbers anyone can type.

This tool runs entirely in your browser. It does not upload your file, your location, or your pulse. It does not watch the camera, the microphone, or the keyboard. Location is added only if you press a clearly labeled button. Body signals are added only if you type them or choose a CSV. Nothing here is a government ID, a face unlock, or a detector of humanity.

AI can invent every field. The honest use is a common container and the habit of recording process: drafts, hours, collaborators, the take that cost something. For the longer argument, the schema, and how this sits beside Content Credentials and proof-of-personhood systems, read the specification.

Place — opt-in only

This page will not read your location unless you ask. A typed place name is enough. Coordinates are optional and stay in the file you download, not on a server.

Body signals — opt-in only

Watches and phones do not hand a static website your Health data. Type a summary, or upload a simple CSV with a header row: timestamp,hr,steps. Optional fourth column: gsr or eda. These values are self-attested. They are not signed by a device, and sweat is not proof of a soul.
